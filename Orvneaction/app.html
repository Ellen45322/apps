Information handling

The app does not transmit personal data to the developer. Content created in the app is stored locally on the device and remains under the user's control.

The app does not require an account and does not maintain a developer-operated user profile.

Analytics, advertising and online services

The app does not use advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics, crash-reporting or cloud-sync services.

Device permissions

The app may request the camera, the photo library. Permission is used only when the related feature is selected and can be changed in iOS Settings.

Third-party services

No third-party service receives personal data from the app.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored content remains on the device until the user deletes it in the app or removes the app. Removing the app normally removes its local data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used where information is processed. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
